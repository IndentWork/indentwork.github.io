Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

IndentWork ("we") builds Kory, an assistant used by therapy centers to manage sessions. This policy explains what information Kory handles and why.

Who decides about the data

Each therapy center that uses Kory decides which children and families it records. We process that information for the center, to run the service.

What we handle

  • Names and mobile numbers of parents, guardians, teachers and center staff.
  • Children's first names (and a nickname the center uses), the therapies they attend and their session times.
  • Records of sessions, cancellations and replacement sessions.
  • Messages sent about sessions, and the Yes / No answers to them.

Why we use it

  • To show the center its timetable, its children and its teachers.
  • To help the center find a new time when a session is cancelled.
  • To send parents short messages about their own child's sessions (for example on WhatsApp or SMS), and to record their answer.
  • To let people sign in with a one-time code sent to their phone.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only the center that recorded it, and the parent or guardian for their own child. Each center sees only its own information. We use service providers to host the service and to deliver messages; they may use the information only to provide that service.

How long we keep it

As long as the center uses Kory, or until the center or the parent asks us to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your choices

A parent can ask the center, or us, to see, correct or delete their information, or to stop messages. Contact us using the details below.

Security

Access requires a sign-in code sent to the person's phone, and every request is checked so that a center sees only its own information.
