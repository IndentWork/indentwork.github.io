Being built · early access for therapy centers

Kory runs the busy part of your therapy center's day.

Kory is an AI assistant for pediatric therapy centers in India. It keeps every child's sessions on track, finds a new time when someone cannot come, and keeps parents informed, so the owner can keep teaching instead of juggling calls.

What Kory does

Every child's sessions, on track

Weekly sessions with the right teacher, and a clear count of what each child still needs this month.

A kid can't come

Kory suggests the best new time (same time first, then the closest) and offers the free seat to another child.

A teacher can't come

Kory suggests a teacher who gives the same therapy and is free, so the session still happens.

Parents in the loop

Short messages to parents about their own child, with a simple Yes or No to answer.

Fair to teachers

Warns before a teacher gets too many difficult sessions in a row.

Plain words, few taps

Made for owners who also teach all day: the assistant proposes, the owner taps yes.

How it works

  1. The center sets up its timetable, teachers and children once.
  2. When something changes ("Aarav can't come on Wednesday"), the owner tells Kory in one tap.
  3. Kory finds the best option and asks the parent if needed. The owner only decides when it matters.

Messages to parents

Kory sends messages only to parents of children enrolled at a center that uses Kory, and only about that child's sessions (for example a changed time, or an offered session). We never send advertising. Parents can ask the center to stop messages at any time.
